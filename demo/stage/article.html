48 hours in Fes: getting lost in the old medina

By a travelling student · 6 min read

The old medina of Fes is a maze of more than nine thousand narrow streets. Don't be afraid to get lost: every shopkeeper will happily point you back to the main gate.

Start your morning with mint tea and fresh msemen near Bab Boujloud. In the afternoon, climb to the terraces above the tanneries for the best view of the city.

Prices are rarely written down, so bargaining is part of the fun. Smile, take your time, and always say thank you, even when you decide not to buy anything.

If you only remember one tip, remember this one: the best food in Fes is not in restaurants, it is sold from small carts in the street.
